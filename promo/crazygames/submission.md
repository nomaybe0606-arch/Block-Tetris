# Grid Pulse

## Short description
Find your flow in a luminous block puzzle. Place colorful shapes on an 8×8 grid, clear rows and columns, and build glowing combos at your own pace.

## Full description
Grid Pulse is a relaxing block placement puzzle with a bright Neon Glass look. Drag shapes from a tray of three onto the 8×8 board. Complete a full row or column to clear it and make room for your next move. Plan ahead, clear several lines together, and keep your streak going to build a higher combo and beat your personal best.

There is no countdown and no falling blocks. Every shape keeps its orientation, so finding the right space is the challenge. Glossy blocks, light trails and colorful clear effects celebrate each good move. Sound, music, intense effects and reduced motion can be adjusted in settings. The game includes English and Russian text.

The CrazyGames Basic Launch build has no ads or purchases. Progress and personal bests use the CrazyGames Data module when it is available; an unavailable host or storage service does not prevent playing the game.

## How to play
Make room for your next great move! Fit shapes onto the board and fill an entire row or column to clear it. Clear multiple lines together for a bigger score, and keep clearing to build a combo. A new set appears after you use all three shapes. The round ends when none of your remaining shapes can fit. Aim for a new personal best, with no time limit.

Desktop controls:
- Mouse: click and hold a shape or its area beneath the board, drag it onto the grid, then release to place it. The preview shows the exact drop position.
- Keyboard: press 1, 2 or 3 to select a shape; use the arrow keys to move its preview; press Enter to place it or Escape to cancel.
- Settings button: pause and adjust audio, language and effects.

Mobile controls:
- Touch and hold a shape or its area beneath the board, drag it onto the grid, then lift your finger to place it.
- Settings button: pause and adjust audio, language and effects.

Shapes cannot be rotated. An invalid placement returns the shape to the tray.

## Devices
Designed for desktop, laptop, tablet and phone browsers. Supports mouse, keyboard and touch. The portrait board stays square and centered in a wide iframe. Chromium gameplay was tested locally; Chrome launch was confirmed, but its interactive automated test could not complete. Native Edge and physical iPhone/Android QA remain release checks (see CRAZYGAMES_RELEASE.md).

## Features
- 8×8 board, three shapes at a time, row and column clearing.
- Multi-line bonuses, combos and personal bests.
- Neon Glass blocks, light trails and clear effects.
- No timer, falling blocks, purchases or Basic Launch ads.
- English/Russian text, optional audio and reduced motion.
- Automatic progress storage through CrazyGames when available.

No global leaderboard, multiplayer, external login or social sharing is implemented.
